# 24 September 2026

LT-FH exercise, following the liability-model lecture of 22 September. Students simulate
a disease register of about 50,000 people in 50 independent groups, describe it with
plots, score everyone with LT-FH++ using
[ltpred](https://github.com/bvilhjal/ltpred), and compare the scores with the true
genetic liability. 15 questions, 12 with plots: most plotting code is given and students
interpret the results; in six cells they complete short expressions. We do not expect
students to finish all questions in class, but encourage them to
complete the remaining questions as homework before October 8.

[Open the exercise in Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/bvilhjal/ATIG_2026/blob/main/teaching_days/2026-09-24/LTFH/LTFH_exercise.ipynb)
