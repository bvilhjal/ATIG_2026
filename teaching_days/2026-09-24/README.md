# 24 September 2026

LT-FH exercise, following the liability-model lecture of 22 September. Students simulate
a disease register, describe it with plots, score everyone with LT-FH++ using
[ltpred](https://github.com/bvilhjal/ltpred), and compare the scores with the true
genetic liability. 14 questions, 11 with plots: most plotting code is given and students
interpret the results; in five cells they complete one expression. Parts 0, A and B in
class, C to E as homework.

[Open the exercise in Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/bvilhjal/ATIG_2026/blob/main/teaching_days/2026-09-24/LTFH/LTFH_exercise.ipynb)
(nothing to install; choose *File → Save a copy in Drive*).
