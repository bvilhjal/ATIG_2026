# 29 October 2026

LD score regression practical: heritability and genetic correlations from published BMI,
triglyceride and blood-pressure GWAS, using [PLDSC](https://github.com/bvilhjal/PLDSC).
Six questions, about 60 minutes.

[Open in Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/bvilhjal/ATIG_2026/blob/main/teaching_days/2026-10-29/LDSC/LDSC_practical.ipynb)
and choose *File → Save a copy in Drive*.

- [Exercise](LDSC/LDSC_practical.ipynb) ([HTML](https://bvilhjal.github.io/ATIG_2026/teaching_days/2026-10-29/LDSC/LDSC_practical.html))
- [Answers](LDSC/LDSC_practical_answers.ipynb) ([HTML](https://bvilhjal.github.io/ATIG_2026/teaching_days/2026-10-29/LDSC/LDSC_practical_answers.html))
- [`run_exercise.py`](LDSC/run_exercise.py) runs the calculations without Jupyter.

The GWAS files (about 260 MB) are downloaded from the GWAS Catalog when the notebook runs; sources and checksums
are in [`LDSC/data/sources.json`](LDSC/data/sources.json).
